# Your Plotus workspace

Choose **Start project** at [plotus.app](https://plotus.app), then sign in with Google or Facebook. The web workspace lives at `/projects` and requires sign-in. Your workspace starts empty and shows the projects saved to your account.

## Start with a project

Select **New project**, enter its name and location, and choose whether it is for a client. Client projects can include the client's name, email and phone number.

Choose **Create project** to save it. Your projects update automatically across devices. Return to Projects to see the latest changes immediately.

## Set up the plot

Creating or opening a project takes you to **Plot setup**:

1. **Boundary:** enter side lengths in feet and inches or metres. Add sides for an irregular plot, delete added edges when the plot has at least five sides, supply measured diagonals when available, or drag the corners. Typed measurements update the drawing and area. Dragging corners adjusts angles while preserving every side length. Use the canvas menu to change units.
2. **Direction:** move the north slider to match the site's orientation.
3. **Roads:** select road-facing sides. Priority appears when more than one side is selected.

On iPhone, **Plot setup** and **Floor plan** appear above the canvas when no section is selected. The canvas stays visible above **Boundary**, **Direction**, **Roads** and **Upload plot image**. Selecting a section highlights it and shows scrolling controls on the same screen. The header and preview compress to make room for typing. Use the keyboard-dismiss button in the top bar to close the keyboard. The three buttons and upload action hide while a section is selected. The top bar shows the selected section. Use **Save** to persist changes; the back arrow returns to the main canvas and keeps unsaved edits. Drag corners in the main view or Boundary to change angles without stretching measured sides; Direction and Roads keep the outline fixed. Measured diagonals lock the angles. On desktop, use the tabs beside the canvas; **Floor plan** saves the plot and continues to the planning brief.

Use **Save** in the project header to keep a draft. Undo and Redo let you revisit edits. Errors appear above the controls and beside the affected measurement. Reopening the project restores the saved plot on iPhone or web.

Side lengths can fit more than one outline. Add measured diagonals or adjust the corners to match the surveyed boundary.

If another device saves a newer version, your local edits remain available. You can keep editing or choose **Reload saved plot** to replace them with the saved version.

## Start from a sketch

Choose **Upload plot image** and select a photo of a hand-drawn or printed plot sketch. Plotus reads annotated dimensions, north and road-facing sides. Cancel photo selection to return without uploading anything. While a sketch is being read, **Cancel** lets you stop waiting and choose another image. Choose **Review measurements** to inspect and correct the extracted outline. Unclear measurements remain blank. Your existing plot stays intact until you choose to apply the result, and extracted values are saved only when you choose Save.

## Plan the building

Select **Floor plan** after completing the boundary and road-facing sides.

1. Choose **Setbacks** from the two buttons below the canvas. Pick Auto or Custom. In Custom, enter All sides, then choose **Different setback for each side?** to adjust individual edges. Select the building’s floors. Change feet/inches or metres in the canvas toolbar. The section name and Save stay in the top bar. Back returns to the main canvas without saving or discarding edits.
2. Choose **Requirements** for BHK and **Vastu compliant**. Pick Auto or Custom on the Requirements row. Custom provides one room list; parking, lift and staircase are added from the same Add room catalogue. Room controls have standard room types without a name field.
3. Save the requirements or choose **Generate plan**. You can return to Projects while generation runs and reopen Floor plan to check the result. A result contains a drawing for each returned floor. A no-fit result keeps the requirements available for adjustment.

The computed buildable area is saved with the project and reused on iPhone and web. Changing floors, BHK, Vastu or rooms keeps the same footprint. Only plot or setback changes require a new calculation.

Room dimensions are automatic in this milestone. Some catalogue entries remain marked Coming later, and the engine reports unsupported combinations before generation. If the boundary or direction changes, review and save setbacks again before generating. A floor-plan concept still needs a designer’s review.

## Keep your work organised

- Search projects by name or location.
- Sort by name or recent activity.
- Open a project to work on its plot. Choose **Edit project** in the project's actions to change its name, location or client details.
- Use the project's actions to delete it when it is no longer needed.
- Change light, dark or system appearance in **Settings → General**.
- View your name and email, manage passkeys, or sign out in **Settings → Account**.

If a save fails, your entered details stay in the form so you can try again. When another device changes a project, return to Projects and reopen it before saving over that newer version.

## Explore the demo

Choose **View demo** on the iPhone welcome screen to explore local sample projects without an account. Demo changes last for that session and stay separate from your saved workspace.
